## Glowing Bars 1.2.0

### New

- **Gathering**: a new reminder type, *Missing tracking*, with a turquoise glow. A miner or a herbalist with no tracking
  on sees Find Minerals or Find Herbs glow. Any tracking counts, so a hunter tracking beasts is left alone.
- **Glow animation** (Settings page): *Circling* (as before), *Breathing* (the glow stands still and slowly fades out and
  in), or *None* (it stands still, fading in and out only as it comes and goes).
- **Faded bars** (Settings page): Action Bars 2 to 8 can be faded until their buttons glow, so a bar in the middle of the
  screen shows only what's due. A glowing button shows in full. The faded opacity can be set (50% by default).
- **Gamepad support (alpha)**: the glows also show on Forever's own gamepad bars, including the class button (a
  paladin's aura). Round buttons get a round glow, with their own animation setting. It's new, so please report what
  you see.

### Fixed

- The glows could stop for good after a fight: in combat the game keeps the buttons' size secret, and the addon stumbled
  on it. They now keep working.
- Closing the `/gb` table with Escape could make the game block its next action (clearing your target, opening the game
  menu) with a "blocked an action" message. The table now closes on Escape without touching the game's own handling.
- An aura you cancel out of combat now glows even while you have a target. Before, it could be taken as still on for
  half an hour.
- A spell you added by hand keeps its own row in the table even when a list has it as another type, so it can still be
  styled or taken out.
