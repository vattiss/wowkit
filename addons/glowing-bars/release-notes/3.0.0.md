## Glowing Bars 3.0.0

### New

- The settings now have a window of their own, with tabs: Welcome, What's New, General, Reminders, Cooldown Manager, Bars, Messages and Help. Open it with /gb settings, with the Settings button on the table, or from Options → AddOns → Glowing Bars. It closes when a fight starts, like the table.
- The Welcome tab opens once on each character after the update. It checks that Glowing Bars finds your buttons, and lets you choose how it uses Blizzard's Cooldown Manager.
- New "Glow on macros" switch under This Character. Turn it off to have only the buttons of spells and items glow.
- The Cooldown Manager tab has a button that opens Blizzard's Cooldown Manager.
- A trinket can now be added as a Ready cooldown by its use spell ID. It glows when the trinket is ready again.

### Changed

- The glow on the Cooldown Manager's icons is now off by default. Switch it on for each window on the Bars tab.
- Only buffs, cooldowns and procs glow on the Cooldown Manager's icons. Old ranks, low reagents and low stock glow only on your buttons.

### Fixed

- Opening the Glowing Bars settings no longer blocks Blizzard's action bar settings in a fight.
- Macros: Glowing Bars now reads the right macro, also one with a blank name, and only the lines that cast something. A macro like /target no longer glows because it has a buff's icon.
- A cooldown set to "Until done" now stops glowing when you use a higher rank of it, like Fade.
