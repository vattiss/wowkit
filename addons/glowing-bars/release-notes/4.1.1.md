## Glowing Bars 4.1.1

### Fixed

- A weapon now holds your class's weapon buff (a poison, a shaman's imbue, a Firestone or Spellstone) and a stone or oil at once, and Glowing Bars keeps them apart: an oil glows next to your Firestone, a stone next to your poison, each until it is on.
- A rogue's macro with a poison on one mouse button and a stone on the other glows for whichever is missing, each by its own row's look and When.
- A weapon buff in its last seconds is asked for again; its time left was misread.
- Proc rules (beta): a spell whose rules all have a group shows only its group rows on the Procs tab, and its own switch there no longer counts. The Proc rules tab's switch column is called Enabled, as in the other tables.
