## Glowing Bars 4.2.0

### New

- Before: set how long before a buff, weapon buff or seal runs out it starts glowing, in the table's Below column on the Buffs tab (/gb). Type it as 15s, 30m or 1h; leave it empty for once it is gone. Set it on the Defaults row for the whole type, or on a row for one spell. The usual times stay the defaults: buffs once they are gone, Battle Shout 3 seconds before, weapon buffs 30 seconds, seals 1.5 seconds.

### Changed

- A stone or oil you added as a weapon buff glows 30 seconds before it runs out too, like your class's weapon buffs (change it with its Before).

### Fixed

- A spell added as a low reagent whose tooltip names the reagent on a new line (a mage's teleports and their Rune of Teleportation) no longer glows with the reagent in your bags.
