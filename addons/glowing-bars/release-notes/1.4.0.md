## Glowing Bars 1.4.0

### New

- **EllesmereUI support**: the glows show on EllesmereUI's action bars. Its bars 1 to 8 count as Action Bars 1 to 8, so
  the bar switches and the faded bars work on them too; its bars 9 and 10 glow, but have no switch of their own.
- **Spell IDs in tooltips** (Settings page, off by default): every spell tooltip shows its ID, and the tooltip of an item
  with a use (an elixir, a potion, food) shows the ID of the spell it casts. Handy to add a spell by its ID in the `/gb`
  table.
