## Glowing Bars 3.2.0

### Fixed

- The old spell rank message no longer comes for a spell on a hidden bar, or on a bar that doesn't glow.

### Changed

- The old spell rank message now names the spell.
- What's New no longer opens by itself after an update. To see it after new features, tick "Show what's new after new features" on the What's New tab, or under All Characters on the General tab.
- To keep the Welcome tab from opening by itself on new characters, untick "Show the Welcome tab on new characters" at the bottom of the tab, or under All Characters on the General tab.
- The Defaults button asks before it resets a tab.
