## Glowing Bars 4.6.0

### New

- Debuffs tab (beta): switch on Use debuff reminders at its top. A debuff of yours the enemy you target lacks glows (purple), when it's in range and you can cast it. Hunters get Serpent Sting and Hunter's Mark, priests Shadow Word: Pain, warlocks Corruption and Curse of Agony; add more by their Spell ID. In a fight it's worked out from your casts.
- Proc rules (beta): a new Buff at stacks condition. Paladins get Unsealed Wrath glowing at 3 stacks, on its Cooldown Manager icon too.
- Proc rules (beta): a new In range condition, for a spell that should glow only when the target is in its range (Feral Charge out of its dead zone).

### Fixed

- Proc rules: emptying a rule's group clears it, and a rule of yours switched off can be switched on again.
