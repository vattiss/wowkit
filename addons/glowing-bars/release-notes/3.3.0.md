## Glowing Bars 3.3.0

### New

- Classic glow looks: every animation now also comes as classic (Blizzard's older gold proc border, in your reminder's color) and original (in the game's own gold). Pick them in the table's Animation column.
- A chat line when a buff on you is an older rank than you know, naming the highest rank you can cast. Switch it off on the Reminders tab, separately for your own buffs and for buffs from others.

### Fixed

- A buff cast at an older rank, such as Mark of the Wild, no longer glows as missing in combat.
- Turning the Cooldown Manager on or off no longer causes an error in combat. It now happens at your next reload or login, and the Cooldown Manager tab says when it's waiting.
- In gamepad mode, the settings' dropdowns no longer cause "blocked an action" messages.

### Changed

- Shorter tooltips.
