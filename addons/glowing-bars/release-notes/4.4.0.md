## Glowing Bars 4.4.0

### New

- Shadowmeld glows as a utility cooldown when it's ready. If you added it yourself, it now uses the built-in row and keeps your settings.
- A chat note when a buff the game hides in a dungeon or raid can't be followed to its end yet (a switch on the Reminders tab).

### Fixed

- In a dungeon or raid, a buff the game hides from addons (food buffs, elixirs, an Arcane Censer's) no longer glows while it is up. It glows once its time runs out, if Glowing Bars saw it before or knows how long it lasts, also after a /reload. Fights in the open world work as before.
- After dying, a tracking (Find Minerals, Find Herbs) glows again right away, also when you wake into a fight.
