## Glowing Bars 4.5.0

### New

- Profiles: your settings now live in profiles, one for each character, kept for your whole account. Nothing to do: each character's profile is made of what it had. Profiles, at the top left of the settings (/gb settings), copies another character's settings into yours, resets yours to the defaults, or deletes the profile of a character you deleted.
- Added spells, at the top left of the table (/gb): the spells added on all your characters. Add or remove one on this character, or see which characters have it and delete it from all of them.
- ConsolePort: its gamepad bar and crossbar buttons are found by themselves after every login or reload, with no scan. A switch on the General tab gives its crossbar buttons a round glow, like ConsolePort's own.
- When your buff's button glows because party or raid members miss it, a message now says who (Power Word: Fortitude missing on: Anna, Bob). It is on by default, whatever the table's Message column says; switch it off on the Reminders tab, for a party and for a raid.

### Changed

- A rogue's poison in the bags counts even when it is on no button: it is listed in the poison picks and told of in the message. The automatic pick still prefers a poison on a button, or the one you put on last.
- Bar addons that make their buttons after the login are found as your key bindings change.
- The slash commands are /gb (the table) and /gb settings; any other word opens the table.
- Going back to an older version keeps your settings, apart from changes to added spells made meanwhile.
