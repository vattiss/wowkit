## Glowing Bars 1.5.0

### New

- **ElvUI support**: the glows show on ElvUI's action bars and its stance bar. Its bars count as the Blizzard bars on
  the same slots (its bar 6 is Action Bar 2), so the bar switches and the faded bars work on them too.
- **Repeat the sound** while a glow is on (missing buffs, weapon buffs, tracking, old ranks): every 5, 10, 30 or 60
  seconds, set in the `/gb` table. The message comes again with it. Off by default.
- **The buff you use glows**: of a group (a paladin's aura, a mage's armor, a shaman's shield, a hunter's aspect, a
  seal, a weapon buff) the one you put on last glows. Before your first one, the one your main talent tree wants (a
  Protection paladin: Devotion Aura, Kings, Seal of Righteousness; a Frost mage: Ice Armor).
- **Seals and Battle Shout glow before they run out**, in time to cast them again.
- **Party blessings by class**: a party member is asked for the blessing you last gave its class; before that, Wisdom
  for a class with mana, Kings (or Might) for the others.
- **Raids**: the group buffs (Prayer of Fortitude, Gift of the Wild, Arcane Brilliance) cover the raid around you in
  Forever, so the whole raid counts, with the number of members missing past five. A paladin's blessings only on the
  classes you gave one in that raid, with the Greater Blessing for one member too.
- **Greater Blessing for one party member**: a switch on the Settings page (off by default).
- **Low reagent limit**: 5, 10, 20 or 40 or fewer, on the Settings page.
- **Weapon buffs can be switched off** in the `/gb` table, one by one; the next one takes its place.

### Changed

- The low reagents are named on the screen when you arrive in a town, no longer in the chat; that message is on by
  default and can be switched off in the table.
- The reminder settings come before the action bars on the Settings page.

### Fixed

- Rogue poisons and warlock stones: the highest one on your bars that you have glows, or the spell that makes the
  stone; no more two ranks glowing at once, and nothing for a stone you cannot make (no Soul Shard).
- A member with your Greater Blessing of Light or Salvation is no longer asked for another blessing.
- Knowing both ranks of Prayer of Fortitude or Gift of the Wild, only the reagent of the higher rank counts.
