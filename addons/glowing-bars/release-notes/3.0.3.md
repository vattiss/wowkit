## Glowing Bars 3.0.3

### Fixed

- Buffs you got from a player outside your group, like Mark of the Wild from a passing druid, no longer glow as missing in a fight.
- Tracked Bars of the Cooldown Manager now has its own Show/Hide on the Cooldown Manager tab, apart from Tracked Buffs. It can also glow and fade, from the Bars tab.
