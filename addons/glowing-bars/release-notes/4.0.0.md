## Glowing Bars 4.0.0

### New

- The table (/gb) has tabs: Buffs, Cooldowns, Procs, Consumables, Old ranks, Sounds and Help. A tab shows only if you have something on it or can add to it, and the table remembers the last one you opened.
- Help tab: what each column does, every choice of its lists, every reminder type, and what to add on which tab, under which type and by which ID.
- Utility cooldown, a new type for the cooldowns between fights: Cultivation, Cannibalize, Rapid Regeneration, Read Leyline, Skysight, Walk on Air, Astral Recall, and the Hearthstone when it is on a bar. It has its own colour and glows Always by default. What you set on these spells' own rows moves with them.
- Skyborne racials: Read Leyline, Skysight and Walk on Air glow when they are ready.
- New When choices: Always + start, Out of combat + start (at the end of a fight), Ally targeted (a living friend, you included) and Any targeted (anyone living).
- Each spell that takes a reagent has its own row on the Consumables tab, named after the reagent and the spell, e.g. "Sacred Candle (Prayer of Spirit)". Your switch, limit and look of the old reagent row move to each of its spells.
- Spells you haven't learned yet are left out of the table. A "Show unlearned spells" switch on each tab brings them back.
- The add row says which ID the type you picked takes (Spell ID or Item ID), with a short hint on what goes under it.
- A warning line under a tab's defaults names the spells whose own default your change of their type took over (e.g. Shield Block, in combat only by default), so you can press Reset on them to keep their own.
- Every paladin seal is listed in the table, the unlearned ones faded.

### Changed

- A ready cooldown glows once: when it becomes ready, while its When allows it. It no longer lights up again at every pull, after every fight, or for a new target. It glows again only when its When begins: the + start choices at the start (or end) of a fight, and the targeted choices for a new target.
- Enemy targeted and Any targeted now hold through the whole fight, so losing your target in combat doesn't turn them off.
- What you set on the Ready cooldown type row (glow %, When, sound, repeat, animation, colour) no longer applies to Cultivation, Astral Recall, the Hearthstone and the other spells that moved to Utility cooldown: they take the Utility cooldown type's defaults. Set them again on the Utility cooldown row if you want them.
- A change to a type now also reaches the spells with a default of their own (Shield Block, Templar's Bulwark, Defensive Execute, the totem elements). Such a spell shows it is changed, and its Reset gives its own default back.
- The addon makes far less garbage on each check, so its memory climbs much more slowly.

### Fixed

- Ready cooldowns no longer glow after a reload as if they had just come back.
- Getting off a mount, leaving a taxi or an inn no longer makes a ready cooldown glow again.
- Read Leyline no longer glows in combat right after you cast it: the Cooldown Manager's icon showing its buff was taken for "ready".
- The Hearthstone and trinkets added by their use spell are no longer shown faded in the table as unlearned spells.
- A spell you learn at a trainer while the table is open shows up without reopening it, and a new spell with ranks appears on the Old ranks tab without a reload.
- The Help and Sounds tabs keep the window's width and are readable in both views.
