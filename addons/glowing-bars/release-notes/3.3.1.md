## Glowing Bars 3.3.1

### Fixed

- Ready cooldowns set to Always no longer stop glowing when a fight starts.
- A paladin's own blessing and aura now glow even when another paladin's different blessing or aura is on you. The same blessing or aura from another paladin still counts, as they don't stack. In a party or raid, a member who already has another paladin's same blessing is no longer named.

### Changed

- Ready cooldowns now glow when a fight starts, for everyone. To switch it off, untick "Glow ready cooldowns when a fight starts" on the Reminders tab.
