## Glowing Bars 1.3.0

### New

- **Bartender4 support**: the glows show on Bartender4's bars, its stance bar (a paladin's auras) included.
- **Dominos support**: the glows show on Dominos's bars, its stance bar included.
- With either one, its bar N counts as Action Bar N: the switches of the bars that glow, and the bars faded until they
  glow, work on its bars too.
