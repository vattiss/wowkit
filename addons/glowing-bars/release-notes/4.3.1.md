## Glowing Bars 4.3.1

### Fixed

- In a fight, a buff that falls off or runs out (Lightning Shield, a totem's buff, any of them) glows again right away, not only once the fight ends.

### Changed

- The 4.3.0 change for buffs in a dungeon or raid is taken back while it gets a better fix: a buff the game hides from addons there (food buffs, elixirs, an Arcane Censer's) may glow while it is up again, as before 4.3.0.
