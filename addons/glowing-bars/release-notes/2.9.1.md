## Glowing Bars 2.9.1

### New

- Touch of Weakness (Undead) and Shadowguard (Troll) are now on the priest list. They're off by default, so switch them on in the Enabled column of the table if you use them.

### Changed

- New characters now start on "Add what Glowing Bars needs", also with EllesmereUI. Characters you've already played keep their choice. With EllesmereUI, the buffs and cooldowns it adds show on its bars, and you can change this in the Cooldown Manager section of the Options page.

### Fixed

- Touch of Weakness added by hand now counts its full 10 minutes in a fight.
