## Glowing Bars 4.1.0

### New

- Proc rules (beta): a new Proc rules tab in the table (/gb), before Procs. Switch on "Use advanced proc rules (beta)" at its top, and procs are lit by rules: a spell and one condition each. Off by default: until you switch it on, procs work as before.
  - Conditions: Usable, Buff up, a target's creature type (Undead, Demon, Beast...), After block, After dodge, After parry, Target dodged (warriors), Free now, Instant now, Target health below and My health below a percent.
  - Prio: the lowest number is checked first, and the first number where a rule holds decides. Result: Glow lights the spell, No glow keeps it dark.
  - Group: rules with the same group share a row, look and switch on the Procs tab, e.g. "Revenge (dodge)" with its own colour.
  - The addon's built-in procs are rules too: switch one off and add your own to change it.
  - The ID and CDM columns show each rule's IDs, and whether the Cooldown Manager can tell its buff in a fight (Full access puts it in Tracked Buffs; a /reload makes it work).
  - The Help tab explains it all.
- A Discord invite on the settings Help tab: come and talk about Glowing Bars.

### Changed

- Arcane Missiles glows for Missile Barrage only: by its buff, or in a fight by its icon in the Cooldown Manager. It no longer glows when Clearcasting made it free. Without full Cooldown Manager access it may not glow in a fight. To light it on Clearcasting too, add a rule on the Proc rules tab.
- Templar's Bulwark at low health has a row of its own on the Procs tab, "Templar's Bulwark (low health)". Your look and switch for it moved there.
- The Procs tab always shows the "Show unlearned spells" switch.
