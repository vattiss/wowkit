## Glowing Bars 3.1.0

### Fixed

- A macro with #showtooltip or #show glows again only for the spell it shows, like before 3.0. A macro without them glows for every spell it casts. A spell for another stance or form doesn't count.

### Changed

- A setting under This Character, "Glow for every spell in a macro", makes #showtooltip and #show macros glow for every spell they cast.
- Macro glows follow a stance or form change faster.
