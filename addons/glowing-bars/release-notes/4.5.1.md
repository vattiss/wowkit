## Glowing Bars 4.5.1

### Fixed

- After a switch of specs or a reset of your talents, the spells of the other spec are no longer reminded (a priest's Shadowform in the Discipline spec) until a /reload.
- A spell with ranks that also has a proc or cooldown row (a warlock's Shadow Bolt) is listed on the Old ranks tab, so its old rank reminder can be switched off.
- A spell name your client writes two ways (with another apostrophe, as in some French names) counts as one spell: one row on the Old ranks tab, and its ranks and buffs are recognized.
