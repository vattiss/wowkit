## Glowing Bars 3.6.0

### New

- Shamans: when a totem you dropped is killed or runs out, it glows, so you can drop it again. It shows the totem that went, not which ones to drop. By default it glows for 3 seconds; set its Glow time to Until done to keep it glowing until you drop it again. Each element (Fire, Earth, Water, Air) has its own row and colour in the table.
- Paladins: Exorcism glows when an undead or a demon is targeted.
- Paladins: Templar's Bulwark glows in a fight at 10% health or below.

### Fixed

- A buff the game refuses with "A more powerful spell is already active", like a low Blessing of Might at a camp with a Lodestone, no longer keeps glowing: after the refusal it stays quiet until one of your buffs is gone.
