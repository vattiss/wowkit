# Rank Reminder

A World of Warcraft: Forever addon (TOC `16001`) that names every spell on your action bars that is a lower rank than
the one you know, so a new rank never stays unused on a bar by accident. It never casts anything.

- A message at the top of the screen, for example *Smite: Rank 1 is on your action bar, but you know Rank 2.*
- It is shown when something is new, and again after 30 seconds if nothing changed.
- Quiet while dead, mounted, on a taxi, eating, or (by default) resting in a town or an inn.

Settings (per character): *Options > AddOns > Rank Reminder*: disable on this character, stay quiet in towns, and the sound the message plays (Alarm clock 1 by default).
Only Blizzard's own action bars are supported.

The smallest addon of the repository (one notice service, one Settings page): the template for a new addon, see
[docs/packages.md](../../docs/packages.md).
